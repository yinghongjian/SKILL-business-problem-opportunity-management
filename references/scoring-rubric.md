# Scoring Rubric

## Value Level

| Level | Definition | Typical Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| S | Company-level impact on revenue, profit, cash flow, hero products, supply chain, platform compliance, or major customer experience | 爆款断货, 核心渠道明显下滑, 重大库存积压, 平台处罚, 集中质量客诉 |
| A | Department core target impact that needs owner resolution this week | 上新延误, 活动报名遗漏, 重点详情页未完成, 退款率异常 |
| B | Local project or efficiency impact | 单款主图待优化, 局部素材问题, 单流程卡点 |
| C | Low-priority record or routine issue | 格式错误, 普通信息记录, 轻微协同提醒 |

Rules:

- S-level does not automatically mean P0.
- P0 does not automatically mean S-level.
- Similar problems repeating within two weeks may be upgraded by one value level.
- Items involving hero products, inventory, delivery, quality, platform penalties, or major customer complaints start at A unless evidence proves lower impact.

## Urgency Level

| Level | Definition | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Clear loss or missed window if not handled today | Same-day boss and owner alert |
| P1 | Must be handled within 48 hours | Push owner and track daily |
| P2 | Should be handled this week | Enter weekly follow-up |
| P3 | Observation or low urgency | Record only |

## Solution Quality Score

Score out of 100:

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Accurate problem/root-cause positioning | 15 |
| Data or fact support | 15 |
| Concrete action | 20 |
| Clear owner | 10 |
| Clear deadline | 10 |
| Expected result or validation metric | 10 |
| Risk fallback plan | 10 |
| Reusable SOP/method value | 10 |

Low-score rules:

- Vague advice such as “加强跟进” without action, owner, time, or metric cannot exceed 40.
- A solution without source problem/evidence cannot exceed 60.
- A cross-department solution without coordination owner cannot exceed 75.

## Achievement Score

Score out of 100:

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Achievement importance | 25 |
| Result clarity | 20 |
| Data/evidence | 20 |
| Business value | 20 |
| Reusable value | 15 |

Achievement levels:

- S: clear company-level result or risk removal.
- A: clear department core target contribution.
- B: local project improvement.
- C: routine completion.

## Evidence Rules

High-confidence evidence includes SKU, platform, category, date, quantity, amount, percentage, before/after comparison, screenshot reference, owner, deadline, or source report.

Set `evidence_strength`:

- `strong`: direct data or concrete fact supports the conclusion.
- `medium`: specific business fact exists but lacks numeric proof.
- `weak`: mostly subjective description; request clarification before high rating.
